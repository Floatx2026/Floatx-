SPV share class structures

Four routes to the same position — different risk, rights and cost profiles

Layer 1 · Top-tier share-holding vehicle

Directly holds the unicorn shares

  • Directly holds the unicorn shares
  • Appears on the cap table as the registered holder
  • Has whatever voting, information and consent rights attach to those shares
  • Signs the SPA, deals with ROFR, gets the company's transfer consent
  • Is the single legal shareholder from the unicorn's perspective
Fees & economics

Direct ownership, direct rights

Access fee 8% – 12%
  • On issuer share register as holder of record
  • Requires ROFR process and transfer approval
  • Net to position: ~88% – 92%
  • GP lock-up: issuer standard (180 days)
Level 1.5 · Intermediary

Forward contract tracking the underlying shares

No share transfer. The existing holder enters a participation interest or forward contract with the SPV. Economic return tracks shares 1:1.

Access fee 13%+
Key features

Speed over registration

  • Holder remains on cap table — no transfer
  • Bypasses ROFR — no issuer approval needed
  • Net to position: ~87%
  • GP lock-up: issuer standard (180 days)
L2 + side letter · Hybrid

L2 access with L1-equivalent rights

Investor subscribes at L2 but a side letter grants L1-equivalent rights — cap table visibility and transfer rights — bridging the two structures.

Fee profile Included in L1 fee
Key features

The best of both

  • Side letter grants L1-level economic rights
  • Requires separate issuer acknowledgement
  • Bridges L2 access with L1 protections
  • Performance fee: 10%–20% above hurdle
Layer 2 · Lower-tier feeder

Feeder hanging off L1 — economics only

  • Holds an interest in L1, not in the unicorn
  • Passes through economics only — dividends, sale proceeds, liquidity events
  • No voting rights in the unicorn — voting stays at L1
  • This is where the downstream investors subscribe
  • Can be one of several L2s feeding into the same L1
Fees & economics

Pooled access, smaller tickets

Management fee 1% – 2% p.a.
  • May carry additional management fee / carry
  • Used for smaller tickets or pooled access
  • Performance fee: 10%–20% above hurdle on liquidation
Layer 3 · Multi-layer wrapper

Fund of funds of funds

An L3 structure wraps the position in three or more entities: the issuer's L1 SPV, an intermediate feeder, and a further fund-of-funds that ultimately holds the LP interest. Most heavily intermediated route.

Layered fees Compounded
Key features

Maximum intermediation

  • 3+ entities between investor and cap table
  • Multiple management fees stack across layers
  • No visibility to underlying holder of record
  • Used where direct access is not available
